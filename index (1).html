Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no credits, plans or watermarks. Transcription runs on your own device. AI features use a free Puter account, which you sign in to once.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Cutting, captions and export all happen in your browser. Only transcript text goes to the AI when you use a smart feature.

Can I paste a YouTube or Instagram link?

Not directly. Browsers are not allowed to download from those sites. Save the video to your device first, then upload it. Direct file links (.mp4, .webm, .mp3) do work.

How long can my video be?

Videos up to about an hour work well on a laptop. On a phone, 20 to 30 minutes is safer because decoding uses memory.

Which languages work?

Transcription supports 34 spoken languages including Hindi, Tamil, Bengali and English. AI can translate captions into 22 languages.

Can Katri post to YouTube or Instagram for me?

No. Auto-posting needs approved platform apps and a server. Katri builds a calendar with a reminder and the ready-to-paste caption for every post instead.

Why does export take as long as the clip?

The browser records the captioned video as it plays, so a 30 second short takes about 30 seconds. Keep the screen on while it records.
